About Me

👋 I'm a UX designer with a uniquely diverse background. My journey started in the skies as a flight attendant for a five-star airline, where I developed exceptional empathy and mastered the art of anticipating needs before they were expressed—skills that now shape my user-centered design approach.

✨ Bridging digital and physical worlds, my experience spans retail design, digital experiences, and content creation. I even built a YouTube channel with over 1 million views, sharing insights on health, fitness, and career growth. This journey taught me how to simplify complex ideas and engage audiences effectively.

🚀 Today, I merge all these perspectives to craft intuitive, user-friendly digital experiences that truly connect with people. My approach blends deep empathy, clear communication, and strategic thinking to tackle challenges with elegant, delightful solutions.
