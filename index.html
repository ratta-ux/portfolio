03 About

From the skies to screens

I'm a UX designer with an unusually diverse path. My career began in the skies as a flight attendant for a five-star airline, where I learned to read people, anticipate needs before they're spoken, and stay graceful under pressure — the exact instincts that now shape my user-centred design work.

"Great design is empathy made visible — anticipating what people need before they ask."

Bridging physical and digital worlds, my experience spans retail design, digital products and content creation — including a YouTube channel with over one million views on health, fitness and careers. That taught me how to simplify complex ideas and hold an audience's attention.

Today I merge all of these perspectives to craft intuitive digital experiences that genuinely connect with people — blending deep empathy, clear communication and strategic thinking.
