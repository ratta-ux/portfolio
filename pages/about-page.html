About me

From the skies to screens.

Hey, I'm Ratta Kidakarn — a product designer who loves building engaging experiences. My curiosity for UX design sparked while I was working as a flight attendant, and it grew into a career of turning empathy into products people love.

Chapter one — five-star airline

01 My journey

Five chapters, one designer

The unusual path that taught me empathy, resilience and craft — scroll through the story.

Chapter 01

The beginning

While flying with a five-star airline, I became fascinated by UX design. It was tough at first — balancing my job and learning design tools and principles during days off and after long-haul flights — but the spark wouldn't fade.

Chapter 02

The learning journey

I started with YouTube tutorials, then enrolled in a design boot camp in the USA. The time difference meant waking up at 3 a.m. for mentor calls — a schedule only passion could sustain.

Chapter 03

First design projects

From paper wireframes to Figma, I learned by doing — even conducting quick user interviews at Starbucks, offering people a free drink for their time. That scrappy, hands-on approach produced my first end-to-end design project, and I'm still proud of it today.

Chapter 04

Growth as a designer

Years in the industry shaped me into a designer who bridges digital and physical spaces across consumer and enterprise products — bringing human-centred design to the forefront and applying strategic thinking to complex problem spaces.

Chapter 05

Giving back

Today I mentor and speak at design events, teaching the next generation of designers — because the ups and downs of my own journey taught me lessons worth sharing.
