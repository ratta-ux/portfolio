Overview

The Vocab Go project was created to address the challenge of learning a new language, which can be difficult and time-consuming. To create an effective solution, the UX design process was applied by following the design thinking methodology, which included five stages: Understand, Define, Ideate, Prototype and Test.

Role:

  • Lead designer

Tools:

  • Figma
  • Miro board
  • Excel sheet

Problem

Learning a new language often lead to frustration and difficult, it requires commitment, repetition, and time. This case study explores the experience of learning and understanding words by carefully researching how people learn language.

Key Pain Points

  • Difficulty remembering vocabulary after initial learning
  • Lack of context for new words makes them harder to retain
  • Existing apps don't provide enough repetition or practice
  • Learning feels tedious rather than engaging
  • Hard to track progress across multiple learning sessions

Solution

100% task completion rate: All participants can easily complete the given task.

User interview & findings

With the problem space explored, it was time to understand the needs and behaviors of the user.To kick things off, I conducted 5 user interviews with people who had tried to learn a language in the past to understand how people approach language learning.

Photo from Unsplash

Participants

  • Used language learning apps within the last 3 months.
  • Know at least a second language, which they use for work, college, or any daily activity.

Interview questions

  • What is your motivation to learn a new language?
  • How do you learn a language from the beginning?
  • Could you walk me through the last time how you learn a new vocabulary?
  • What is your opinion about a vocabulary learning app?
  • What do you think about vocabulary learning (offline/online)?
  • Tell me about a moment when you felt frustrated while learning a new language?
  • What makes you happy during the study process?

Findings

  • Many users feel frustrated while learning a new language when they cannot remember the words that they have gone through many times.
  • Many users feel happy when communicating in a second language with their friends.
  • Many users feel that learning through applications encourages them to continue their studies more.
  • Many users believe they can get more money if they can speak more languages.

Persona

Based on the interviews with 5 people, I set up one persona to help clarify my user demographics, needs, goals, emotions, behaviors, fears, and challenges.

By bringing my users' perspectives, I was able to restate my project brief in the form of user stories, to stories, and a problem/hypothesis statement.

Lisi
"I believe that if I can speak more languages I can get more money."

Challenges faced by persona

Lisi has limited knowledge of the Japanese language. Thus, she struggles to communicate using the Japanese language at work.

Problem statement

Lisi (a freelance model) needs an easy-to-use vocabulary app with a minimal design concept in which she could listen to audio scripts spoken by native speakers, and expand her vocabulary in Japanese by using flashcards.

We will know this to be true when we see Lisi communicating in Japanese at work.

Hypothesis

We believe that by building an intuitive vocabulary learning app with essential Japanese phrases, various words using flashcards, and features using the repetition method, we will help Lisi achieve her goal of speaking Japanese fluently and with the proper pronunciation.

Usability test

To start validating my design decisions as quickly as possible, I conducted 5 usability tests with the low fidelity prototype. Once the gathered feedback, it allows me to assess if the app is useful. This can be confirmed by asking users to perform a series of direct and scenario tasks, subsequently recording my findings.

Test plan

SCOPE: This test will check how people do flashcard quizzes, search for the word, and create their flashcards with the Vocab Go app. Each session will run for about 10-15 minutes.

Test result

The prototyping, testing, and refinement process was repeated several times. Each time measuring the usefulness of that current iteration.
