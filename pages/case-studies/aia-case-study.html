Outcome

Conversion rate increased

The iPad app significantly improved conversion rates for agents by providing a centralized platform to store and access data effectively.

Close sale faster

The user-friendly interface made it easy for new agents to learn and navigate the app without additional in-person training.

Onboarding time reduced

The "real-time" tool - made quotations streamlined processes, minimized time spent on submission, tracking, and closing sales, and made it easier to track applications.

UI design principles

Clarity

Make the next step obvious and easy to complete.

Efficiency

Remove unnecessary steps for completing a task.

Invisibility

Make your product so easy to use that the user doesn't notice design elements.

Consistency

Make your product consistency then user will not get lost.

Bio

Agent represents both new and old agents who are currently using a mix of digital and paper methods to sell insurance.

Goals
  • Streamline the sales process by consolidating data storage and usage.
  • Use an app that is user-friendly and meets expectations.
  • Close sales faster by generating real-time, tailor-made quotations and tracking applications.
Pain points
  • Cannot create real-time, tailor-made quotations within the app, leading to delayed approvals.
  • Difficulty tracking applications from submission to closing the sale.
  • Struggles with managing data across various storage methods (paper, Google Drive, Excel, etc.)
  • Unable to sign within the app, causing delays in closing deals compared to competitors.

What will I do differently

1. Allocate more time for usability testing in the project timeline. The more we test and gather user feedback, the more we can refine and enhance the user experience.

2. Set a way of working with the developer team right from the start. This way, I can reduce misunderstandings and make sure everyone is on the same page, leading to smoother project execution.
