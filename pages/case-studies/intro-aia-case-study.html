02 The problem

How do you make buying insurance faster, easier, and truly digital?

Traditionally, buying health insurance was a tedious experience — agents arrived with a briefcase full of documents, requiring clients to sift through pages, sign multiple forms, make copies, and then wait days for approval.

This outdated process was inefficient, time-consuming and prone to errors, creating friction for both agents and clients.

03 The solution

From briefcase to one tap

As the lead designer, I reimagined the agent–client journey end to end — replacing stacks of paperwork with instant personalised quotes, in-app signatures and real-time application tracking, working closely with research, product, engineering and leadership.
