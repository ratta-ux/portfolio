AIA HEALTH INSURANCE

AIA Health Insurance App

Revitalizing user engagement: An insurance sale tool for agents and clients.

AIA Group Limited, Asia's largest life insurer, is a Hong Kong-based multinational insurance and finance corporation comparable to Manulife Financial, Canada's largest insurance provider.

02/

Problem

Agents didn't use sale tool on iPad. iPad is part of the selling insurance in front of the client. However, agents avoided the existing sales tool due to its inefficiencies and limitations, so they used alternative methods for managing customer data and sales processes.

The inefficient workflow and lack of digital tools were causing delays in closing deals compared to competitors, difficulties in tracking applications, and challenges in creating real-time quotes for clients.

Quotation

Cannot create real-time quotations

Agents cannot create real-time, tailor-made quotations within the app, leading to delayed approvals.

Tracking

Difficult to track applications

Agents struggle to track applications from submission to closing the sale.

Data

Complex data management

Struggles with managing data across various storage methods (paper, Google Drive, Excel, etc.)

Signature

No digital signature

Unable to sign within the app, causing delays in closing deals compared to competitors.

03/

Solution

Here is the design solution that addresses the key pain points identified during research:

Sidebar navigation

Quick access to key features - Enabling agents to navigate efficiently
Minimize cognitive load - Clear categorization of functions
Improved navigation - Consistent navigation pattern throughout the app
Before

Agents had no tracking feature and had to call the office or recheck emails to determine the status of applicants, which was time-consuming and inefficient.

Solution

I introduced sidebar navigation that allows agents to easily track the status of applications and maintain consistency with the main app.

Personalization insurance plan

Personalization - Tailored insurance offerings to client needs
Reduced training time - Intuitive interface requires less training
Faster process - Streamlined workflows for creating quotes
Before

Agents could only sell select products through the app, while customized products required time-consuming paperwork and approval from the office.

Solution

Now agents can personalize products and quotations within the app, eliminating the need to visit the office to submit hard copies.

Signature sign in app

Cost and resource savings - Eliminates paper documentation
Error reduction - Minimizes human error in documentation
Legally binding - Digital signatures meet compliance requirements
Before

Agents had to submit hard copies of documents to the office to initiate the sales process and obtain approval.

Solution

I introduced a digital signature feature that enables agents to take photos of documents and easily upload them directly into the app.

04/

Outcome

The results of implementing the new insurance app

Conversion rate increased

The iPad app significantly improved conversion rates for agents by providing a centralized platform to store and access data effectively.

Close sale faster

The user-friendly interface made it easy for new agents to learn and navigate the app without additional in-person training.

Onboarding time reduced

The "real-time" tool made quotations streamlined processes, minimized time spent on submission, tracking, and closing sales, and made it easier to track applications.

UI design principles

Clarity

Make the next step obvious and easy to complete.

Efficiency

Remove unnecessary steps for completing a task.

Invisibility

Make your product so easy to use that the user doesn't notice design elements.

Consistency

Make your product consistency their user will not get lost.
