02 The problem

How do you transform education access with a multi-language reading platform?

Access to quality educational materials is often limited for many children, especially in underserved communities. LearnBig was created to bridge this gap by providing open e-books and e-texts, ensuring learning is accessible to all.

With over 1,000 resources in Thai, Myanmar, Malay and English, users can download books, comics, government textbooks, teacher guides, exercises and multimedia resources — all categorised for easy access.

03 The solution

A library without borders

As the lead designer, I shaped the library, search and reading experiences — making more than a thousand books easy to discover across four languages, for readers of every age and ability.
