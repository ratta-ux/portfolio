How do you transform education access with a multi-language digital reading platform?

Access to quality educational materials is often limited for many children, especially in underserved communities. Learn Big was created to bridge this gap by providing open e-books and e-texts, ensuring that learning is accessible to all. With over 1,000 educational resources in Thai, Myanmar, Malay, and English, users can download books, comics, government textbooks, teacher guides, exercises, and multimedia resources—all categorized for easy access.
