How do you transform wholesale shopping into a seamless digital experience?

During the pandemic, when in-person shopping became a challenge, Makro saw the need to bring its wholesale experience online. To meet this demand, they hired me to design and build their first mobile app, making bulk shopping more accessible for businesses and households.

03/

The Solution

As the lead designer, I owned the search, homepage, My List, and checkout experiences, collaborating closely with user research, product, design, engineering, and leadership to create a frictionless, high-performing shopping experience.
