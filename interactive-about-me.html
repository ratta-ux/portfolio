About Me

I'm a UX designer with over 5 years of experience creating digital products and retail experiences. My background in customer-facing roles has given me a unique perspective on how users interact with both digital and physical spaces.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me exploring Vancouver with my French Bulldog Bao, trying new restaurants, or working on my photography skills. I believe that great design should feel personal, and I bring that philosophy to every project.

My approach combines user-centered design principles with a passion for creating meaningful experiences that feel intuitive and delightful.
